# Security policy

secbox is an early build. Do not rely on it to contain anything yet. [What works today](guide/src/start/status.md) and [`docs/PROTECTIONS.md`](docs/PROTECTIONS.md) say what has and has not been shown.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report it privately, through **Report a vulnerability** on this repository's Security tab. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you did, what happened, and the version or commit. Reports of a way out of the sandbox, a secret or SSH key reaching a sandbox, or an approval that can be bypassed are the most useful.

There is no bug bounty, and no fixed response time yet.
